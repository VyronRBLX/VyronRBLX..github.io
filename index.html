01Requirements

I need a clear 'to-do' list before I start. If you want to change things once I've started coding, we'll treat those as new tasks with a new price.

02Payment policy

50% upfront, 50% on completion. The price we agree on is final, and you are responsible for covering any transaction taxes/fees.

03Refunds

No cancellations once confirmed. Upfront payments aren't refundable. I only provide refunds if I am personally unable to finish the task.

04Bug fixes

I fix my own bugs for free! Please report issues within 24 hours. After that, any problems are assumed to be from your own edits.

05Ownership

You get the files and full rights after the final payment. I keep the right to show the work here in my portfolio as a showcase.

06Ghosting

If you don't respond for 3+ days, the project may be canceled without a refund. Please keep communication lines open!
